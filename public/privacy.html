PRIVACY POLICY

Your Privacy Matters

We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how we collect, use, and store your data.

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Dr. Mohammed Al Amin ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our personal brand website at malamin.cc. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring that your personal data is handled securely and in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

Data We Collect

We collect the following types of personal information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, company name, and job title when you submit booking requests, collaboration inquiries, or questionnaires.
  • Booking Information: Session type, preferred date and time, and any notes or context you provide when booking a call.
  • Collaboration Requests: Type of collaboration, organization details, relevant links, and your message when submitting collaboration inquiries.
  • Questionnaire Responses: Your answers to the ten-question partner matching questionnaire.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and referral source for security and analytics purposes.

How We Use Your Data

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • Processing Requests: To handle your booking requests, collaboration inquiries, and questionnaire submissions.
  • Communication: To send you confirmation emails and respond to your inquiries.
  • Service Improvement: To improve our services and website functionality based on usage patterns.
  • Security: To protect against fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access to our systems.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for the following periods:

  • Bookings: 12 months from the booking date, or as required for business purposes.
  • Collaboration Requests: 12 months from submission date.
  • Questionnaire Responses: 12 months from submission date.
  • Admin Sessions: Sessions automatically expire after inactivity.
  • Technical Logs: Limited period for security and troubleshooting purposes.

After the retention period, your data is securely deleted or anonymized.

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal or business requirements.
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service provider.
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent for data processing where applicable.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@malamin.cc.

Security Measures

We implement robust security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption: All data is transmitted using HTTPS encryption.
  • Password Hashing: Admin passwords are hashed using scrypt for secure storage.
  • Session Management: Secure session-based authentication with HTTP-only cookies.
  • Database Security: Parameterized SQL queries to prevent injection attacks.
  • Regular Audits: Periodic security reviews and vulnerability assessments.
  • Cloudflare Protection: DDoS protection, WAF, and bot detection via Cloudflare.

Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience and for security purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for admin session management and website functionality.
  • Cookie Consent: We request your consent before using non-essential cookies.
  • Storage Duration: Cookie consent is stored for 1 year.

You can manage your cookie preferences through our cookie consent popup or by adjusting your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare: Content delivery network (CDN), DDoS protection, and web application firewall (WAF). Cloudflare may process your IP address and basic request data for security purposes.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: Bot protection service that analyzes user behavior to prevent automated abuse. Turnstile may collect device fingerprinting data.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Third-party services are used only for the purposes described above and are bound by their own privacy policies.

Data Transfers

Your data is stored on servers located within our hosting provider's infrastructure. We take appropriate measures to ensure your data remains secure during transfer and storage.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor, we will take steps to delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on our website and updating the "Last Updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: info@malamin.cc
  • Website: https://malamin.cc
